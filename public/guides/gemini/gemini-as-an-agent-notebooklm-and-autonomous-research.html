What is Gemini as an Agent — NotebookLM and Autonomous Research?
Using Gemini's agentic surfaces for autonomous research and knowledge work

Gemini's agentic surface combines two flagship Google features: NotebookLM (the source-grounded notebook AI) and Deep Research (the autonomous research agent). Together they form Google's most distinctive agentic offering — useful for any research-heavy or knowledge-management workflow.

Best for: research teams, content creators, students, consultants, and anyone whose work involves synthesising large amounts of source material.

04
Deep Research Max
Autonomous web research

Give it a topic and Deep Research plans a multi-step strategy, executes against the web, and returns a structured report. New in 2026: uploads as sources, MCP integration, and Canvas rendering of results.

  • Brief — 'Research [topic]. Focus on [angle]. Use [time period].'
  • Plan — agent shows its research strategy before executing
  • Execute — agent browses, captures, and synthesises
  • Report — structured output with citations
  • Render — convert to interactive Canvas, quiz, or infographic

The research workflow that consistently produces useful output

Deep Research Max is most valuable for genuine research tasks — competitive analysis, literature review, market scans, due diligence. Less valuable for quick factual questions.

  • Brief — 'Research [topic]. Focus on [angle]. Use [time period]. Compare with [comparator].'
  • Plan — agent shows research strategy before executing; review and adjust
  • Execute — agent browses 10-30 sources, captures findings, cross-references
  • Report — structured output with citations, downloadable
  • Convert — render as Canvas doc, interactive quiz, infographic, or audio overview

When each output format works best

NotebookLM's distinctive output formats serve different consumption patterns. Pick the format that matches how your audience will actually engage.

  • Audio Overview — for consumption while doing other work (commute, exercise)
  • Video Overview — for sharing with people who won't read source material
  • Infographic — for quick visual reference; meeting handouts
  • Quiz — for learning/training contexts; verify understanding
  • Standard report — for the analyst who needs to verify claims against citations

Gems Integration
Building agents grounded in NotebookLM

Gems (Gemini's Custom-GPT equivalent) can now connect to a NotebookLM notebook. Build a Gem with a NotebookLM-backed knowledge layer of hundreds of sources, deploy it as a tailored assistant. This is the 'Custom GPT plus deep grounding' pattern.

✅ DO THIS

  • Build a NotebookLM notebook with your domain knowledge (hundreds of sources OK)
  • Build a Gem with the right system prompt and connect to the notebook
  • Use Gems for repeated questions in a domain (customer FAQ, internal policy)
  • Test adversarially before sharing with team
  • Always include an escape hatch — 'I don't know — let me get you a human'
  • Update the underlying notebook as content changes; Gem inherits

❌ AVOID THIS

  • Connecting Gems to disorganised or stale knowledge sources
  • Skipping adversarial testing — public-facing Gems need stress testing
  • Letting Gems handle out-of-scope queries unprompted
  • Mixing multiple distinct domains in one Gem
  • Sharing Gems with broad audiences without quality review
  • Forgetting to update notebooks when underlying domain content changes

What Doesn't Yet Work Well
Honest limits
  • Real-time data — NotebookLM is for stable sources, not live feeds
  • Source freshness — manually refresh sources; no auto-sync from arbitrary sites
  • Multi-language source mixing — works but English remains strongest
  • Very large source sets — quality degrades past several hundred sources
  • Hallucination — even with citations, occasionally synthesises incorrectly; always verify
⚠️ KNOWN LIMITS OF THE 2026 STACK
Real-time data (NotebookLM is for stable sources, not live feeds). Source freshness (manual refresh; no auto-sync from arbitrary sites). Multi-language source mixing (works but English remains strongest). Very large source sets (quality degrades past several hundred). Hallucination (even with citations, occasionally synthesises incorrectly — always verify high-stakes claims).

Common Mistakes & Fixes
What goes wrong — and how to avoid it

Most AI workflows that 'don't work' fall into one of these patterns. The fix is almost always sharper context and stricter review, not switching models or tools.

  • Treating Gemini as an Agent — NotebookLM and Autonomous Research as a finished answer rather than a draft to review
  • Vague prompts that don't specify audience, format, or what you want changed
  • Forgetting to give it context — the file, the brief, the constraints
  • Skipping the verify step — always check numbers, dates, names, and policy claims
  • Using it for tasks where a 30-second human action would be faster

✅ DO THIS

  • Provide context — the file, the brief, the audience, the constraints
  • Iterate; don't just accept the first output
  • Always review numbers, names, dates, and policy claims
  • Treat AI as a fast draft generator, not an answer engine
  • Capture what works so you can replicate it
  • Match the model to the task — fast modes for drafts, reasoning modes for analysis

❌ AVOID THIS

  • Sending AI output without reading it end-to-end
  • Vague prompts with no audience, format, or success criteria
  • Assuming AI can see files or context it hasn't been given
  • Using AI for tasks faster done manually in under a minute
  • Treating numbers, quotes, or citations as verified without checking
  • Defaulting to the slow reasoning mode for every task

Suggested Prompts
Ready-to-use prompts you can adapt to your work

These prompts are designed to land in your real work today. Replace the bracketed placeholders with your actual context — the more specific you make them, the better the output. Treat each one as a starting brief, not a finished script.

NOTEBOOKLM SETUP
I want to build a NotebookLM notebook for [topic]. I have these sources: [list]. Walk me through: how to upload them, what initial questions to ask to test the notebook's quality, and how to know if I need more sources before generating an audio overview.
DEEP RESEARCH BRIEF
Brief Deep Research Max on: [topic]. Specifically, I want to know [specific angle]. Time period: [period]. Use both my NotebookLM notebook for [topic] as a primary source and the public web for fresh updates. Return: structured report with citations and a 1-page executive summary.
COMBINED AGENT
Help me design a Gem that's grounded in our internal knowledge (we have 80 sources in a NotebookLM notebook). The Gem should answer questions about our products, cite our docs, and explicitly say 'I don't know — escalate to support' when the answer isn't in the sources. What's the system prompt?
