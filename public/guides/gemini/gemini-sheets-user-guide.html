What is Gemini in Sheets?

Gemini in Google Sheets helps you work with data faster — whether you're building formulas, analysing trends, cleaning messy data, or creating spreadsheet templates from scratch. It's accessible via the "Help me organise" panel and the Gemini side panel.

🧮 Formula generation

Describe what you want to calculate in plain English — Gemini writes the formula.

📊 Data insights

Ask questions about your data and get analytical answers without building pivot tables manually.

🧹 Data cleaning

Identify duplicates, fix formatting, standardise entries, and transform columns.

🏗️ Template creation

Generate complete spreadsheet structures — budgets, trackers, dashboards — from a brief description.

Access points:

Click the sparkle/Gemini icon in the toolbar to open "Help me organise," or open the side panel via the Gemini icon on the right rail. For formula help, click any cell and describe what you need in the formula bar assistant.

Help Me Organise

"Help me organise" is Gemini's primary interface in Sheets. It generates structured tables and spreadsheet layouts from a plain text description — useful for creating new sheets or adding structure to existing data.

1

Click the sparkle icon

In Google Sheets, click the Gemini icon in the top toolbar to open the "Help me organise" panel.

2

Describe the structure you need

"Create a project tracker with columns for: task name, owner, start date, due date, status (dropdown), and priority." Gemini generates the structure.

3

Insert or refine

Preview the generated structure, adjust if needed, then insert it into your sheet. Add sample data or dropdowns if suggested.

Describe the use case, not just the columns:

"I'm tracking marketing campaign performance across channels. I need to record spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, and CPA for each campaign." Gemini picks the right columns and can add calculated fields automatically.

Formula Generation

Formula generation is one of the most immediately useful Gemini features in Sheets — especially for users who know what they want but don't know the syntax. Describe the calculation in plain English and Gemini writes the formula.

How to use it

Click a cell, open the side panel, and type: "Write a formula that [description]." Gemini returns the formula with an explanation. Copy it into your cell.

Examples

What you wantWhat Gemini writes
"Count all rows where column C says 'Complete'"=COUNTIF(C:C,"Complete")
"Sum column D only where column B = 'Marketing'"=SUMIF(B:B,"Marketing",D:D)
"Calculate the % change from last month to this month"=(C2-B2)/B2*100
"Look up the price for a product code in column A from a price table on Sheet2"=VLOOKUP(A2,Sheet2!A:B,2,FALSE)
"Flag rows where the due date has passed and status is not Complete"=IF(AND(B2"Complete"),"OVERDUE","")
Explain a formula you've found:

Paste any formula into the side panel and ask "Explain what this formula does step by step." Gemini breaks down complex nested formulas into plain English — great for inheriting someone else's spreadsheet.

Data Analysis & Insights

The Gemini side panel can analyse your spreadsheet data and answer analytical questions — without you building pivot tables or charts manually.

What you can ask

  • "What month had the highest total revenue in this sheet?"
  • "Which product category has the best margin percentage?"
  • "Is there a correlation between ad spend and conversions in this data?"
  • "Summarise the key trends in this data for a non-technical stakeholder"
  • "Which rows have the largest variance from the average?"
  • "What would you recommend I look into further based on this data?"
Ask for a chart recommendation:

"What's the best way to visualise this data? Suggest a chart type and explain why." Gemini will recommend the right chart type for the data pattern and explain how to create it in Sheets.

Analytical limitations

Gemini analyses the data it can see in your sheet. For very large datasets (100,000+ rows), it may work from a sample. For statistical significance or predictive modelling, use dedicated tools — Gemini's analysis is descriptive and analytical, not predictive modelling.

Cleaning & Transforming Data

Messy data is a universal problem. Gemini in Sheets can identify issues and write formulas to clean and standardise your data — tasks that would otherwise require manual sorting or complex formula knowledge.

Common cleaning tasks

🔤 Standardise text

"Write a formula to capitalise the first letter of each word in column A" → =PROPER(A2)

🗑️ Remove duplicates

"Find and highlight duplicate values in column B" — Gemini explains the conditional formatting approach or UNIQUE formula.

✂️ Split columns

"Split the full name in column A into first name and last name in separate columns" — Gemini provides the SPLIT formula.

📅 Fix dates

"Convert the text dates in column C (format: DD/MM/YY) into proper Google Sheets date format" — Gemini writes the DATEVALUE conversion.

Audit your data first:

Before cleaning, ask: "Look at column B and describe any inconsistencies or formatting issues you notice." Gemini scans the data and flags problems you may have missed — missing values, mixed formats, outliers.

Creating Templates

One of the most time-saving uses of Gemini in Sheets is generating complete spreadsheet templates from a description. Instead of building from scratch, describe what you need and insert a ready-to-use structure.

Template types Gemini builds well

  • Budget trackers — income vs expenses, monthly summaries, variance analysis
  • Project trackers — tasks, owners, timelines, status, dependencies
  • KPI dashboards — metric tracking with target vs actual columns
  • CRM light — contact management, pipeline stages, follow-up dates
  • Inventory trackers — stock levels, reorder points, supplier info
  • Event planning — guest lists, budget allocation, task checklists
Be specific about the business context:

"Create a monthly marketing budget tracker for a small e-commerce business with £50k/month spend. Include channels (Paid Social, SEO, Email, Events), planned vs actual spend, and a variance percentage column." The more context, the more useful the template.

Advanced Techniques

Combine formula generation with data context

Describe your sheet structure when asking for formulas: "My sheet has: Column A (Date), B (Product), C (Units Sold), D (Unit Price). Write a formula in column E that calculates total revenue, and another in a summary row that shows monthly totals." Gemini writes both formulas correctly because it knows the structure.

Build Google Apps Script via Gemini

For repetitive tasks, ask Gemini to write Apps Script code: "Write a Google Apps Script that runs every Monday morning and sends an email to [address] with a summary of all rows in the sheet where Status = 'Due This Week'." Gemini writes the full script — paste it into Tools → Script Editor.

Create named ranges with explanations

Ask: "What named ranges should I set up for this budget sheet to make formulas easier to read?" Gemini recommends meaningful named ranges and explains how they'll simplify future formula writing.

Always verify generated formulas:

Gemini's formula generation is excellent but not infallible — particularly with complex nested formulas or when cell references need to be adjusted. Always test on sample data before applying to your full dataset.

See It In Action

A marketing analyst asks Gemini to generate a campaign performance formula and explain the insights from their data.

Google Sheets — Gemini Side Panel
"My sheet has: A=Campaign, B=Channel, C=Spend, D=Clicks, E=Conversions. Write a formula for column F that calculates Cost Per Conversion, and flag it red in column G if CPC is over £50."
Gemini Response
Column F — Cost Per Conversion:
=IF(E2=0,"N/A",C2/E2)
Column G — Flag:
=IF(AND(F2<>"N/A",F2>50),"⚠️ HIGH CPC","")
Column F divides Spend by Conversions. The IF prevents division by zero errors. Column G flags any row where CPC exceeds £50 with a warning label.
