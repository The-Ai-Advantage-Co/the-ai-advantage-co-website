Gemini Extensions & Workspace

Connect Gemini to your Google apps — Drive, Gmail, Maps, YouTube, and more — to get answers grounded in your actual data.

1

What are Extensions?

Extensions connect Gemini to external services and your personal Google account data. Without extensions, Gemini only knows what's on the public web and what you tell it in the conversation. With extensions enabled, Gemini can read your Drive files, scan your Gmail, find places on Maps, summarise YouTube videos, and search flights — all in a single conversation.

📁

Google Drive

Search and summarise files in your Drive — Docs, Sheets, Slides, and PDFs.

📧

Gmail

Find and summarise emails, extract information, and understand your inbox without leaving Gemini.

🗺️

Google Maps

Find places, get directions, check hours, and plan routes through natural language.

▶️

YouTube

Summarise videos, get key points, and find relevant content without watching entire videos.

✈️

Flights

Search and compare flight options using Google Flights data inside Gemini chat.

🏨

Hotels

Find and compare hotel options for a destination via Google Hotels search.

How to enable extensions:

Open Gemini → click the Extensions icon (jigsaw piece) in the toolbar → toggle on the extensions you want. Extensions are linked to your Google account and require you to authorise access.

2

Google Drive Extension

The Drive extension lets Gemini search your Google Drive files and answer questions about their content. It works with Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, PDFs, and other supported file types.

What you can do

  • Find files by content, topic, or keywords: "Find documents about the Q3 marketing plan"
  • Summarise long documents: "Summarise the key points from my project proposal doc"
  • Extract specific information: "What was the budget figure in the contract I have in Drive?"
  • Compare files: "How does the March report differ from the April report?"
  • Answer questions across multiple files: "What do my client briefs have in common?"
Be specific with file references:

Include file names, approximate dates, or distinctive phrases to help Gemini locate the right file. If Drive contains many files with similar names, specify: "the Q4 report from October 2024, not the Q3 one."

Privacy and permissions

When you enable the Drive extension, you authorise Gemini to read your Drive files to answer questions. Gemini does not store your file contents — it accesses them only during your conversation session. You can revoke access at any time via Google Account settings.

3

Gmail Extension

The Gmail extension gives Gemini access to your inbox — enabling it to search, summarise, and extract information from emails without you having to manually copy content into the chat.

Powerful use cases

📬 Email catch-up

After time away: "Summarise the most important emails I received this week and flag anything that needs a response."

🔍 Information retrieval

"Find the confirmation email for my flight to Sydney and tell me the departure time and booking reference."

📋 Thread summary

"Summarise the email thread with [contact name] about [project] — what's been agreed and what's still open?"

📊 Pattern analysis

"How many invoices from [supplier] did I receive in the last 3 months? What were the amounts?"

Gmail extension reads — it doesn't send:

The Gmail extension can read and summarise your emails but cannot draft or send emails on your behalf from within the Gemini chat interface. Use the native "Help Me Write" feature inside Gmail for drafting.

Privacy note

Only emails from your own Gmail account are accessible. Gemini does not have access to email attachments — only the body text of emails. Revoke access any time via Google Account → Third-party apps.

Google Maps Extension

The Maps extension brings location intelligence directly into Gemini. Instead of switching to Google Maps, you can describe what you need in plain language and get results in the conversation.

What you can ask

  • "Find me a good Italian restaurant near [location] with outdoor seating"
  • "What are the opening hours for [business name] on Saturday?"
  • "How long does it take to drive from [location A] to [location B] at 9am on a Monday?"
  • "Find the nearest petrol station to [address]"
  • "Plan a walking route through the main attractions in [city]"

Combining Maps with other extensions

The real power comes from combining Maps with Gmail and other data. For example:

Cross-extension prompt:

"I have a meeting tomorrow — find the address in my Gmail confirmation from [company], then tell me how long it will take to get there from [my office location] at 8:30am and find nearby parking options."

This single prompt uses Gmail to find the address and Maps to plan the journey — without switching between apps.

5

YouTube Extension

The YouTube extension lets Gemini summarise video content, extract key information, and find relevant videos — without you watching them first.

Core capabilities

1

Summarise a specific video

Paste a YouTube URL and ask: "Summarise the key points of this video." Great for long lectures, conference talks, or tutorials.

2

Find relevant videos

"Find YouTube videos explaining how to use pivot tables in Google Sheets for beginners." Gemini searches and surfaces relevant results.

3

Extract specific information

"In this video [URL], what does the presenter say about pricing? I only need that section."

4

Compare video content

"Find 3 YouTube reviews of [product] and summarise what reviewers agree and disagree on."

Best for long-form content:

YouTube extension is most valuable for videos over 20 minutes — keynotes, courses, documentary-style content — where the cost of watching the whole video is high. For short videos, it's often faster to just watch.

Flights, Hotels & More

Gemini connects to Google Flights and Google Hotels to enable travel planning directly in chat, combining real-time search data with conversational guidance.

Google Flights

  • "Find the cheapest flights from London to New York in the first two weeks of March"
  • "Compare direct vs connecting flights from Sydney to Tokyo next month"
  • "What's the price difference between flying on Tuesday vs Sunday?"

Google Hotels

  • "Find hotels in Barcelona under £150/night for 5 nights in June, with a pool"
  • "What are the best-reviewed boutique hotels near the Colosseum in Rome?"
  • "Compare Marriott vs Hilton options in [city] for a business trip next week"
Full trip planning in one conversation:

You can plan an entire trip in a single Gemini conversation: use Flights to find options, Hotels to shortlist accommodation, Maps to check distances, and then ask Gemini to draft an itinerary based on your choices — all without leaving chat.

Other available extensions

Google periodically adds new extensions. Check the Extensions panel in Gemini for the latest additions — including third-party integrations that may be available in your region.

Advanced Techniques

Chain extensions in a single prompt

The most powerful use of extensions is chaining them — using multiple data sources in one conversation turn. Example: "Check my Drive for the supplier contract with [Company X], find their contact email in Gmail, and look up their head office location on Maps."

Combine extensions with Deep Research

Run a Deep Research session on a topic, then follow up using Drive: "Based on this research, are there any existing documents in my Drive that relate to this topic?" Gemini bridges external research with your personal knowledge base.

Use natural date references

Extensions understand relative dates. "Last quarter," "this week," "since January," and "next month" all work correctly — you don't need to specify exact dates.

Ask for structured output from extension data

After retrieving data via extensions, ask Gemini to format it: "From those emails, create a table showing sender, date, amount, and status." Structure transforms raw retrieval into usable intelligence.

Privacy mode tip:

If you're working with sensitive Drive or Gmail content and don't need extensions for a particular query, start your prompt with "Without using my Drive or Gmail..." to ensure Gemini answers from general knowledge only.

Suggested Prompts

Drive Summary
"Search my Google Drive for documents related to [project name]. Give me a one-paragraph summary of each file you find, including what stage the project appears to be at based on their contents."
Gmail Catch-Up
"I've been away for [X days]. Summarise the most important emails I've received, grouped by: (1) needs my response, (2) needs no action but I should know about it, (3) can be ignored. Flag any urgent items."
Cross-Extension Research
"Check my Drive for any existing research or documents on [topic]. Then search YouTube for videos on the same topic published in the last year. Give me a combined brief: what I already have and what's new in the video content."
Trip Planning
"I'm planning a trip to [destination] for [dates] with a budget of [amount]. Find me: (1) the best value flights from [city], (2) hotels under [price] per night near [area], and (3) a 3-day itinerary with Maps links for key attractions."
Restaurant Research
"I need to book a dinner for a client in [area]. Find me 3 restaurant options that are: good for business meals, have private dining or a quiet area, rated 4+ stars on Google, and have a menu suitable for dietary restrictions including [requirements]."
Video Learning
"Find the top 3 YouTube tutorials on [skill] for someone at [beginner/intermediate] level. For each video, give me: length, what it covers, and whether the teaching style is more theoretical or hands-on."
