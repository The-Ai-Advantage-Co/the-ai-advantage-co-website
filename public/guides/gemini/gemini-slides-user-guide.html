Generate complete presentations, create custom images, write speaker notes, and refine your decks — with Gemini built into Slides.

1

What is Gemini in Slides?

Gemini is integrated into Google Slides to accelerate presentation creation — from generating a full deck from a brief, to writing speaker notes, creating custom images, and refining slide content. It's available through the "Help me create a presentation" panel and the Gemini side panel.

📽️ Create presentations

Generate a full slide deck with structure, content, and layout from a brief description.

🖼️ Image generation

Create custom images for slides using Imagen AI — no stock photos needed.

🎤 Speaker notes

Generate speaker notes for any slide — from talking points to full scripts.

✍️ Content refinement

Rewrite, shorten, or improve slide content via the side panel or inline tools.

Access:

Open the "Help me create a presentation" panel via the sparkle icon in the toolbar, or use the Gemini side panel for refining existing presentations. Image generation requires Gemini Advanced.

2

Help Me Create a Presentation

The most powerful feature — generate a complete slide deck from a single description. Gemini creates the slide structure, content, layout suggestions, and even placeholder images.

1

Open the creation panel

Click the sparkle "Help me create a presentation" icon in the Slides toolbar.

2

Describe your presentation

Provide: topic, audience, purpose, number of slides, and tone. The more detail, the better the output.

3

Generate and review

Gemini creates the full deck. Review structure and content — each slide is editable like any Google Slides content.

4

Refine slide by slide

Select individual slides and use the side panel to rewrite specific content, add slides, or strengthen weak sections.

Good brief structure:

"Create a 10-slide presentation for [audience] on [topic]. Purpose: [goal — e.g. get sign-off / educate / sell]. Tone: [professional/inspirational/technical]. Must include: [any essential sections — e.g. problem statement, solution, ROI, next steps]."

Adding & Editing Slides

Beyond creating a full deck, Gemini can add individual slides to existing presentations or rewrite specific slide content on demand.

Adding slides

  • Open the side panel and ask: "Add a slide that covers [topic] and fits after the current slide on [subject]"
  • "Create a title slide for this section: [section name]"
  • "Add an agenda slide at the start based on the rest of the presentation"
  • "Create a summary/conclusions slide based on the content of this deck"

Editing slide content

  • Select slide text → right-click → "Help me refine this" for targeted rewrites
  • Side panel: "Rewrite this slide to be more concise — max 4 bullet points"
  • "Strengthen the headline on slide 3 to be more compelling"
  • "Convert this paragraph of text into 5 bullet points"
One idea per slide rule:

Ask Gemini: "Review this deck and tell me which slides are trying to say too much. Suggest how to split them." Strong presentations have one clear point per slide — Gemini can help enforce this discipline.

Image Generation

Gemini in Slides includes Imagen — Google's AI image generator — so you can create custom, original images for your slides without searching stock photo libraries.

1

Insert → Image → Generate image

Via the Insert menu or the side panel: "Create an image of..." Describe what you need.

2

Describe specifically

Include style, mood, composition: "A professional, minimal illustration of a team working collaboratively — flat design, blue and white palette, no faces visible."

3

Choose from options

Imagen generates multiple options. Select the best fit for your slide. Regenerate if none match your needs.

4

Resize and position

Once inserted, treat the image like any Slides image — resize, reposition, and layer with text.

Avoid generating images of real people:

Imagen should not be used to generate realistic images of specific named individuals. Use it for abstract visuals, illustrations, diagrams, scenes, or conceptual imagery.

Speaker Notes

Speaker notes turn a slide deck into a complete presentation tool. Gemini can generate notes for any slide — from bullet-point talking points to full scripts.

Generating notes

  • Select a slide → right-click → "Generate speaker notes"
  • Side panel: "Write speaker notes for each slide in this deck — aim for 3-4 talking points per slide, 30 seconds each"
  • "Write speaker notes for slide 5 that explain the data on the chart for a non-technical audience"

Note types

Talking points"3 bullet points per slide"Experienced presenters who need prompts, not scripts

Q&A prep"What questions might the audience ask about this slide?"Preparing for challenging Q&A sessions

Design & Formatting Help

Gemini can advise on design and structure — even if it can't apply all formatting changes automatically, it can guide you to improve visual consistency and readability.

What Gemini can help with

📐 Structure review

"Review this deck's structure. Does the flow make sense? Where might the audience lose interest?"

✂️ Content reduction

"This slide has too much text — rewrite it as 3 concise bullets that can be read in 5 seconds."

🎯 Headline writing

"Rewrite the slide titles to be action-oriented — each should communicate the key message, not just label the topic."

🖼️ Visual suggestions

"What type of visual would best illustrate the concept on slide 4? Describe what I should create or search for."

Action headlines vs label headlines:

Label headline: "Revenue Performance" — tells the audience nothing. Action headline: "Revenue grew 34% — driven entirely by enterprise accounts." Ask Gemini to convert all your label headlines to action headlines. Your deck immediately becomes more persuasive.

Advanced Techniques

Build from a document

If you have a Google Docs report or strategy document, open Slides and ask: "Based on the following document [paste key content], create a 12-slide executive summary presentation. Focus on: key findings, recommendations, and next steps."

Adapt for different audiences

Build one "master" deck, then ask Gemini to adapt versions: "Rewrite slides 3-7 for a technical engineering audience" and separately "Rewrite slides 3-7 for a board-level financial audience." Same content, different framing.

Create a leave-behind

After presenting, use the side panel: "Based on this presentation, create a one-page executive summary I can send as a follow-up. Include: the problem, our solution, key benefits, and call to action."

Storyboard with Gemini

Before creating slides, ask Gemini in chat: "Help me storyboard a 15-minute presentation on [topic] for [audience]. What's the right narrative arc? What should each section achieve? What visual metaphors work?" Build the story first, then create the deck.

Use Deep Research → Slides pipeline:

Run a Deep Research session → export to Docs → open Slides → "Create a presentation based on this research document." The research becomes the slide content in one workflow.

Suggested Prompts

Full Deck Brief
"Create a [number]-slide presentation for [audience — e.g. board of directors / sales prospects / new employees] about [topic]. Purpose: [goal]. Tone: [professional/conversational/inspirational]. Must include slides on: [list key sections]. Keep bullet points to max 4 per slide."
From Document
"Based on the following report content, create a 10-slide executive presentation. Focus on: the 3 key findings, 2 strategic recommendations, and a clear call to action for the leadership team. [Paste document content or key sections]"
Image Generation
"Create a visual for this slide. The concept is [describe what the slide is about]. Style: [e.g. clean corporate illustration / abstract data visualisation / warm professional photography]. Colour palette: [colours]. Mood: [professional / energetic / calm]. No text in the image."
Speaker Notes
"Write speaker notes for this slide. Format: 3-4 talking points (not a full script). Each point should expand on a bullet, add a concrete example or statistic, and take about 20 seconds to deliver. End with a transition to the next topic."
Content Tightening
"Review all slides in this deck and: (1) convert any label-style headlines to action headlines that state the key message, (2) flag any slides with more than 4 bullet points, (3) suggest which slides could be merged. Be specific."
Q&A Preparation
"Based on this presentation, what are the 10 most challenging questions the audience might ask? For each question, give me: the question, why someone might ask it, and a concise 2-sentence answer I could give."
