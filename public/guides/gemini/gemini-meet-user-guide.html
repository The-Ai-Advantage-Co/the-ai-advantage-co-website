Gemini in Google Meet

Capture every decision, action item, and key point automatically — so you can be fully present in meetings instead of taking notes.

1

What is Gemini in Meet?

Gemini adds AI capabilities directly to Google Meet — live captions with real-time translation, automatic meeting summaries, action item extraction, and smart noise cancellation. These features run during and after your meetings without any manual note-taking.

📝 Meeting summaries

Get an AI-generated summary of the meeting immediately after it ends — delivered to your email and Chat.

✅ Action items

Automatically extracted action items from meeting conversations — who said what and what was agreed.

🌐 Live translation

Real-time caption translation for multilingual meetings — available in 60+ languages.

🔇 Noise cancellation

AI-powered background noise removal — blocks traffic, typing, and ambient sounds in real time.

Availability:

Gemini features in Meet require Google Workspace with Gemini for Workspace enabled. Some features (summaries, action items) require a Workspace Business Standard or higher plan. Live translation is available in all Workspace plans.

2

Live Captions & Translation

Live captions display real-time transcription of everything said in the meeting. Translation adds an additional layer — converting captions into your chosen language in real time.

1

Enable captions

During a meeting, click the "CC" (closed captions) button at the bottom of the screen, or use the "..." More Options menu → Turn on captions.

2

Enable translation

Captions → Settings → Translation language. Choose your target language. Captions will now show both the original speech and your chosen language.

3

Adjust display

Captions appear at the bottom of the screen. You can resize the caption area or pin it. Each speaker is labelled separately.

Use captions for accessibility and focus:

Even in same-language meetings, captions help you follow along when audio quality is poor, when speakers have strong accents, or when you need to refer back to exactly what was said. They also keep you focused — reading and listening simultaneously improves retention.

Supported languages

Google Meet supports live captions in English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, and more — with translation into 60+ languages. Check Google's current language support list as it's regularly updated.

Meeting Summaries

When "Take notes with Gemini" is enabled, Google Meet records a transcript of the meeting and generates a structured summary delivered to participants after the call ends.

Enabling note-taking

1

Start the note-taking

During a meeting, click "Activities" (pencil icon) → "Take notes with Gemini." Meeting participants see a notification that AI note-taking is active.

2

Meeting runs as normal

Gemini transcribes and analyses the conversation. You don't need to do anything — just participate fully.

3

Receive the summary

After the meeting, Gemini sends a summary to the meeting host's email and Google Chat. It includes: key discussion points, decisions made, and action items.

Consent notification is mandatory:

When note-taking is active, all participants see a notification. You cannot secretly record or transcribe a meeting. This is by design — ensure all participants are aware and comfortable before enabling.

What the summary includes

SectionWhat's covered
Meeting overviewParticipants, duration, high-level topic
Key discussion pointsMain topics covered with brief summaries
Decisions madeExplicit agreements and conclusions reached
Action itemsTasks assigned — who is responsible, and any deadlines mentioned

Action Items & Follow-ups

The action item extraction is where Gemini in Meet creates the most immediate business value — turning verbal commitments into a tracked list automatically.

How action items are captured

Gemini identifies phrases that indicate a commitment or task: "I'll send that over by Friday," "Can you take a look at the brief?" and "Let's schedule a follow-up for next week" all trigger action item extraction. Each item includes the speaker's name and the context.

Make your commitments explicit:

Gemini captures better action items when people use clear language: "Sarah will send the report by Thursday" is captured more reliably than "yeah I'll sort it." Encourage explicit ownership language in your meetings.

After the meeting

📋 Review action items

Check the summary email for the full action list. Verify each item is correctly attributed and deadlined.

📧 Share the summary

Forward the summary to team members who weren't present or stakeholders who need visibility.

📅 Add to Calendar

Copy action items into Google Tasks or Calendar events to ensure follow-through with deadlines.

📁 Save to Drive

Copy the summary to a Google Doc for the project folder — builds a searchable meeting history over time.

Smart Features

Beyond transcription and summaries, Gemini adds several smart features to the meeting experience.

Studio features (Workspace Enterprise)

🎨 Studio look

AI-enhanced video — adjusts lighting, skin tone, and sharpness in real time so you look better on camera regardless of environment.

🔇 Studio noise cancellation

Enhanced noise removal that eliminates background sounds including keyboard typing, paper rustling, and ambient conversation.

📡 Adaptive audio

Multiple people in the same room? Adaptive audio prevents echo and audio conflicts when several microphones are active.

📊 Attendance tracking

Automatically logs who attended, when they joined, and when they left — useful for large team meetings and compliance purposes.

Q&A and Polls

Meet's Q&A and Polls features are enhanced by Gemini — after a meeting with active Q&A, Gemini can generate a summary of questions asked and answers given, so presenters can follow up on unanswered questions.

Meeting Preparation

Gemini isn't just useful during meetings — it's a powerful tool for preparing before them and for processing output after.

Before the meeting

Use Gemini (in chat or side panel of Calendar) to help prepare:

  • Agenda drafting: "Draft a 45-minute meeting agenda for a quarterly business review with [team]. We need to cover: performance review, Q4 plans, budget status, and team wellbeing."
  • Briefing notes: "Create briefing notes for a meeting with a new client prospect. They're a mid-market retail company and we're pitching our inventory management solution."
  • Tough question prep: "What difficult questions should I prepare for in a meeting where I'm presenting a project that is over budget?"
Pre-meeting workflow:

1. Use Gemini to draft the agenda → send via Google Calendar. 2. Use Gemini to create briefing notes → save to Google Drive. 3. During meeting: enable note-taking. 4. After meeting: review summary → process action items → follow up via Gmail using Help Me Write.

7

Advanced Techniques

Build a meeting intelligence system

For recurring meetings (weekly team calls, monthly reviews), save each Gemini summary to a dedicated Google Drive folder. Over time you build a searchable record of every decision, action, and discussion — enabling pattern analysis and accountability tracking.

Cross-reference summaries with Gemini

After saving multiple meeting summaries to Drive, use the Drive extension in Gemini chat: "Look at my meeting summaries from the last 3 months for the [project] project. What action items have been mentioned repeatedly that haven't been resolved?" This surfaces systemic blockers.

Use the transcript for detailed review

When note-taking is enabled, a full transcript is available — not just the summary. For complex meetings (legal discussions, technical architecture reviews, client negotiations), download the transcript and ask Gemini in chat: "Review this meeting transcript and identify any ambiguous commitments or areas of disagreement."

Late joiners:

If you join a meeting late, the "catch me up" feature (when available in your Workspace plan) gives you a brief summary of what's been discussed since the meeting started — no more whispering to a colleague.

Suggested Prompts

Agenda Creation
"Draft a [duration]-minute meeting agenda for [meeting type — e.g. quarterly review / project kickoff / client discovery]. Attendees: [roles]. We must cover: [key topics]. Structure it with: time allocations, clear purpose for each item, and a decisions/actions section at the end."
Difficult Meeting Prep
"I'm about to have a meeting with [context — e.g. an unhappy client / a team member about performance / investors asking why we missed targets]. What are the 5 most challenging questions I might be asked? For each, give me a suggested approach to answering."
Briefing Notes
"Create one-page briefing notes for a meeting with [person/company]. Context: [what you know about them]. Our agenda: [topics]. Our goal: [outcome you want]. Include: background on them, key talking points, and what we want agreed by end of meeting."
Summary Processing
"Here is the Gemini summary from today's meeting: [paste summary]. Extract from it: (1) all action items with owners and deadlines, (2) all decisions made, (3) any open questions that need follow-up. Format as a structured follow-up email I can send to participants."
Follow-Up Email
"Write a post-meeting follow-up email based on these notes: [paste action items / summary]. Include: brief summary of what was agreed, action items with owners and deadlines, and next meeting date if applicable. Tone: professional and clear."
Pattern Analysis
"Here are meeting summaries from the last [number] weeks: [paste summaries]. What action items appear repeatedly across multiple meetings? Which decisions have been revisited or reversed? What topics seem to generate the most discussion time?"
