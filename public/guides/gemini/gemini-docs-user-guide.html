Gemini in Google Docs

Write faster, refine smarter, and understand any document instantly — with Gemini built directly into your Google Docs workflow.

1

What is Gemini in Docs?

Gemini is embedded throughout Google Docs — accessible via the "Help me write" prompt bar, the side panel, the toolbar, and inline via the @ menu. It can generate new content, refine what you've already written, summarise long documents, and answer questions about the text — all without leaving the document.

✍️ Help Me Write

Generate a first draft, section, or outline from a brief description — insert directly into your document.

🔄 Refine selection

Select any text and use Gemini to rewrite, shorten, elaborate, or change the tone.

📋 Summarise

Condense any document or section into a concise summary — great for long reports and research.

❓ Ask questions

Use the side panel to ask questions about your document: "What are the key recommendations?"

Access points:

Click the sparkle/pencil icon at the top of a blank doc, use Insert → Gemini, press "@" for inline access, or open the side panel via the Gemini icon on the right toolbar.

2

Help Me Write

Help Me Write in Google Docs generates content directly into your document. It works best for creating first drafts, generating section content, and building structured documents from a brief.

1

Open the prompt bar

Click the sparkle "Help me write" button at the top of the document, or place your cursor and press "@" → "Help me write".

2

Describe what you want

Be specific: "Write a 500-word executive summary for a market entry strategy report targeting retail customers in Southeast Asia."

3

Review and insert

Gemini generates a draft. Click Insert to add it to your document, or Refine to adjust before inserting.

4

Edit and continue

Once inserted, edit the text as normal. Use Help Me Write again for the next section or to fill gaps.

Document-level context:

If content already exists in your document, Gemini can use it as context. Ask: "Based on the section above, write a conclusions section that ties together the main points." Gemini reads your existing content.

Rewriting & Refining

The refine feature is one of the most powerful in Docs — select any text, click the Gemini icon that appears, and instruct Gemini to rewrite it in any way you specify.

Built-in refine options

  • Formalise — makes the selected text more professional and formal
  • Shorten — cuts length while preserving meaning
  • Elaborate — expands with more detail and explanation
  • Rephrase — rewrites with different word choices while keeping the same meaning
  • Custom instruction — type any instruction: "Make this more persuasive" or "Rewrite for a non-technical audience"
Rewrite for a different audience:

Select a technical paragraph and instruct: "Rewrite this for a senior executive who needs the business impact, not the technical detail." This is one of the most practical uses of Gemini in Docs for knowledge workers.

Before/after comparison

Summarising Documents

Gemini can summarise the entire document or specific sections — making it easy to create executive summaries, extract key points, or understand a long document quickly.

How to summarise

  • Whole document: Open the side panel → click "Summarise document" or type "Summarise this document"
  • Selected text: Highlight a section → click the Gemini icon → choose "Summarise"
  • Custom summary: "Summarise the key risks and mitigation strategies mentioned in this document"

Summary use cases

📋 Executive brief

Summarise a 30-page report into a 5-bullet executive brief before a leadership meeting.

📊 Key decisions

"What decisions or recommendations are made in this document?" — extract actionables from meeting notes.

🔍 Document review

Summarise a contract or policy doc: "What are the key obligations for our organisation in this document?"

📝 Review prep

Before editing someone else's document, summarise it first to understand the intent before changing anything.

Q&A on Your Documents

The Gemini side panel turns your document into an interactive knowledge source. Ask questions about the content and get specific answers — without searching manually.

What you can ask

  • "What is the proposed budget in section 3?"
  • "Who is responsible for each action item in this document?"
  • "What are the assumptions underlying the financial projections?"
  • "Does this document mention any compliance requirements?"
  • "What is the timeline for the project phases?"
  • "Are there any conflicting statements in this document?"
Great for document review:

Before approving or signing off on a long document, use Q&A to check: "What does this document commit us to?" and "What are the exit clauses?" — faster and more targeted than reading every paragraph.

Combining with external context

You can add external context in your question: "Given that our standard terms require 30-day payment, does this contract conflict with our policy?" Gemini answers with both the document content and your added context.

Side Panel & Research

The Gemini side panel in Google Docs is a full AI assistant that operates alongside your document. Unlike the inline tools, it supports multi-turn conversation and can bring in web context alongside your document.

Side panel capabilities

💬 Document Q&A

Ask multi-turn questions about the document. Follow up to narrow down to specific sections.

🌐 Web research

Ask Gemini to research a topic related to your document and insert the findings as a new section.

✅ Content review

"Review this draft for logical consistency" or "Check if the conclusions follow from the evidence presented."

📐 Formatting help

"Reformat the next 3 paragraphs as a numbered list" or "Create a table from the data in section 2."

Research within context:

Use the side panel for inline research while writing: "Find the current global market size for electric vehicles and suggest where I should include this data in my document." Gemini points to the right location and provides the statistic.

Advanced Techniques

AI-first document drafting

Instead of starting with a blank page, use Help Me Write to generate a full outline first: "Create a 10-section outline for a business case document for [project]." Then use Help Me Write section by section to fill each part.

Style and tone consistency

If a document has been written by multiple people and the voice is inconsistent, select all and instruct: "Rewrite this document so it has a consistent, professional tone throughout — keep all facts and figures but standardise the language."

Document translation

Select text and use a custom refine instruction: "Translate this section into French, maintaining the professional tone." Useful for multilingual reports without leaving Docs.

Generate structured content from raw notes

Paste meeting notes or bullet points into a document and use Help Me Write: "Based on the notes below [paste notes], write a structured project update document with sections for: Progress, Blockers, Next Steps, and Decisions Made."

Use Docs as a staging area:

For complex AI tasks — writing a long report, developing a strategy document — Docs is better than Gemini chat because you can see and edit the output in context, use refine on specific sections, and maintain document structure throughout the drafting process.

Suggested Prompts

Document Outline
"Create a detailed outline for a [type of document] on [topic] aimed at [audience]. Include suggested headings, the purpose of each section, and approximate word counts. The total document should be around [length]."
Section Draft
"Write the [section name] section of this document. It should: explain [key point], include [specific elements], and transition logically from the previous section on [prior topic]. Tone: [description]. Length: [word count]."
Audience Rewrite
"Rewrite the selected text for a [new audience — e.g. board of directors / general public / technical team]. Preserve all key information and facts but adjust the vocabulary, level of detail, and emphasis to match what matters to that audience."
Document Review
"Review this document and tell me: (1) Are the conclusions supported by the evidence presented? (2) Are there any gaps in the argument? (3) Is the structure logical? (4) What would a critical reader challenge? Be direct."
Key Extraction
"Extract from this document: (1) all decisions made, (2) all action items and who owns them, (3) all deadlines mentioned, (4) any risks or concerns flagged. Format as a structured summary I can share with people who haven't read the document."
Consistency Check
"Check this document for: (1) internal contradictions or conflicting statements, (2) inconsistent terminology for the same concept, (3) sections where the tone shifts abruptly. List any issues you find with the location in the document."
