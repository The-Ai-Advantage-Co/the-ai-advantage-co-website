What is a Great Prompt?

A prompt is everything you send Gemini before it generates a response. The quality of Gemini's output is directly proportional to the quality of your input. A vague prompt produces a generic response. A specific, structured prompt produces targeted, useful output.

Gemini has unique strengths compared to other AI tools: it searches the web in real time, connects to your Google Workspace, reasons across images and documents, and has a very long context window. Great prompting with Gemini means activating these capabilities deliberately — not just asking questions and hoping for the best.

The core principle:

Tell Gemini who you are, what you need, what format you want, and what constraints apply. Then let it use its real-time knowledge and Workspace access to deliver.

2

The Five-Part Formula

Every powerful Gemini prompt contains up to five parts. You don't need all five every time — but knowing each one helps you write better prompts for any situation.

1
Role / Persona
Tell Gemini who it should be or how it should think about the task.
"Act as a senior marketing strategist..."
2
Context
Give the background Gemini needs. Your industry, situation, audience, constraints.
"I work in B2B SaaS, launching a new product in Q3..."
3
Task
Be specific about exactly what you want. Action verb + what + scope.
"Write a 3-paragraph launch announcement..."
4
Constraints
What Gemini should avoid, limits to respect, or rules to follow.
"Under 200 words. No technical jargon. Professional tone."
5
Format
Specify how you want the output structured — bullet list, table, numbered steps, email format.
"Format as a bulleted executive summary."
Gemini tip:

You don't need all five in every prompt. For quick factual questions, the task alone is enough. For complex outputs (reports, strategies, emails), use all five for consistently better results.

3

Gemini-Specific Techniques

Gemini has capabilities that other AI tools don't — and prompting techniques that exploit them.

🌐 Ground in Web Reality

Add "Search the web for current information" or "Based on the latest data" to trigger Gemini's real-time search. This is especially powerful for market trends, news, and current events.

📅 Use Date Awareness

"Today is [date]" anchors Gemini's web searches and helps it filter for relevant, current results. Particularly useful for competitive analysis and news monitoring.

🔗 Reference Your Workspace

Use @Gmail, @Drive, @Docs, @Calendar to pull in real data from your Google account. "Search @Gmail for emails from [sender] this week" is more powerful than any manual search.

🔄 Chain Requests

Build on previous responses in the same conversation. "Now expand section 2 with supporting data from the web" — Gemini maintains context and improves iteratively.

5

Multimodal Prompting

Gemini can process images, documents, audio, and video. The way you prompt for multimodal tasks differs from text-only prompts — you need to anchor the instruction to the content type.

🖼️ Images

Upload then instruct: "Describe what's in this image and identify any issues." Gemini reads charts, screenshots, photos, and diagrams.

📄 Documents

"Read this PDF and extract all action items" or "Compare these two contracts and highlight differences." Supports PDF, DOCX, and text files.

📊 Data

"Analyse this spreadsheet and tell me the top 3 trends." Upload CSV or Excel files — Gemini reads, interprets, and summarises.

🎥 Video (Gemini Advanced)

"Watch this video and summarise the key points." Gemini Advanced can process video content and extract structured information.

Multimodal anchoring:

Always tell Gemini what the file is and what you want from it. "Here's a chart — what trend does it show?" is much more effective than just uploading without instruction.

7

Advanced Techniques

Few-Shot Prompting

Show Gemini examples of the output style you want before asking it to produce. "Here are two examples of the email format I use: [example 1] [example 2]. Now write one for [new situation]." Gemini learns the pattern from examples far better than from abstract descriptions.

Chain of Thought

Ask Gemini to show its reasoning before giving an answer: "Think through this step by step before giving your final recommendation." This forces Gemini to slow down and produce more considered, accurate outputs — especially valuable for complex decisions.

Role + Critique Pairing

Generate, then critique: "Write a draft of [X]." Then: "Now review that draft as a sceptical [audience type] and identify the three weakest points." This two-step pattern consistently produces better final outputs than a single-pass prompt.

Persona Stacking

Combine multiple expert perspectives: "Analyse this plan from three perspectives: (1) a CFO focused on ROI, (2) a CMO focused on brand, (3) a COO focused on execution. What would each one approve and push back on?" Gemini can hold multiple viewpoints simultaneously.

Deep Research integration:

For research-heavy tasks, start with a Gemini Deep Research report, then use that output as context in a follow-up conversation. "Based on the research you just generated, now draft a [deliverable]."

Common Mistakes & Fixes

MistakeFix
One-word prompts ("Write a summary")Add the five parts: who it's for, what's being summarised, length, tone, format
Not specifying output formatEnd every prompt with "Format as [bullet list / table / numbered steps / email]"
Forgetting to use @ for Workspace dataAlways use @Gmail, @Drive, etc. when you want real data — not Gemini's assumptions about your inbox
Accepting the first responseIterate: "That's good — now make it 30% shorter" or "Rewrite section 2 to be more direct"
Not anchoring multimodal uploadsAlways explain what the file is and what you need Gemini to do with it
Using Gemini like a search engineDon't ask "what is X" — ask "explain X to [audience] and give me [specific output type]"

Suggested Prompts

Research Brief
"Search the web for the latest trends in [industry] in [year]. Find 5 credible sources published in the last 6 months. Summarise the 3 most important developments and explain what they mean for [my business type]. Format as a 1-page brief with source links."
Decision Analysis
"I need to decide between [option A] and [option B]. My context: [brief situation]. Analyse both options on: cost, time to implement, risk, and strategic fit. Think through it step by step, then give a clear recommendation with your reasoning."
Document Extract
"Read the attached document and extract: (1) the top 5 findings or decisions, (2) any action items or next steps, (3) dates or deadlines mentioned, (4) any risks or concerns flagged. Format as a structured brief I can share with my team."
Content Rewriter
"Here's a [content type] I wrote: [paste content]. Rewrite it for [audience] using a [tone] voice. Keep the core message but make it more [quality: compelling/clear/concise]. Limit to [word count] words."
Headline Generator
"Generate 10 headlines for [topic/content]. The audience is [audience]. The goal is to [drive clicks / inform / persuade]. Make 3 data-driven, 3 curiosity-driven, and 4 benefit-driven. Don't use clickbait."
Social Content
"Turn this content into social posts for three platforms: LinkedIn (professional, 150 words), Twitter/X (punchy, under 280 chars), and Instagram (engaging caption with relevant hashtags). Source content: [paste content]."
