Kick-Start Pack

Three sessions, one platform, one clear arc. By the end you're not "trying out Ai" — you're using it.

Who it's for

People who want to get genuinely confident with Ai — not just dabble. Pick one platform; we'll take you from setup to applying it to your actual work in three structured sessions.

The three sessions

  • 01 · Discovery & setup — what you do, what's slow, where Ai fits
  • 02 · Application — working real tasks live, building the workflows
  • 03 · Close-out & next steps — what to keep, what to drop, what to try next

How it's delivered

3 × 60 minutes, online or face-to-face. Personalised action plan after session 2. Platform user guides included so you can refer back any time.
